Jarvis Privacy Policy

Jarvis is a personal assistant app used only by its developer. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the developer's permission, Jarvis accesses data from the developer's own accounts, such as Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts) and WHOOP (recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts).

How it is used

Only to provide personal assistance, summaries, and reminders to the developer.

Storage

Data is stored encrypted on the developer's own computer, not on any public server.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared. Content may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate responses for the developer.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from each connected account's settings, and stored data can be deleted from Jarvis.
